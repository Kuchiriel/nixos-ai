# Rest in a dark, quiet room (for headache)

**Condições:** headache, sleeplessness

## Uso

Resting with eyes closed in a dark, quiet room is a recommended non-medication self-care measure during a headache or migraine. Drinking water to prevent dehydration and placing a cool cloth on the forehead are often combined with this rest.

## Como usar

Go to a quiet, darkened room, close your eyes, and rest. Drink water and place a cool damp cloth on your forehead to help ease discomfort.

## Evidência

MedlinePlus (NLM) describes resting in a quiet, darkened room as one of the key things you can do to feel better during a headache or migraine, alongside drinking water and using relaxation techniques. These recommendations are attributed to NIH sources on headache and migraine management.

## Cautelas

Sudden severe ('thunderclap') headache, headache with fever, stiff neck, confusion, or vision changes may signal a serious condition and require emergency evaluation. Frequent headaches that disrupt daily life should be discussed with a healthcare provider rather than managed solely at home.
