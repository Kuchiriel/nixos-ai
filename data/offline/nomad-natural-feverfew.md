# Feverfew

**Também chamada:** Tanacetum parthenium

**Condições:** headache

## Uso

Feverfew is promoted for migraine headache prevention and for minor head and tension pain. Topically, it is marketed for itching and skin irritation.

## Evidência

A 2020 systematic review of seven migraine studies found inconsistent results, though some evidence suggests feverfew may reduce migraine frequency and associated symptoms such as nausea and light sensitivity. There is little or no evidence supporting feverfew for other health conditions.

## Cautelas

Side effects include nausea, digestive issues, bloating, and mouth sores from chewing fresh leaves. People sensitive to ragweed may experience allergic reactions. Feverfew may slow blood clotting and should be stopped at least 2 weeks before scheduled surgery. It may interact with migraine medications and should be avoided during pregnancy due to potential effects on uterine contractions.
